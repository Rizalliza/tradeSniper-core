# News Opening Symbol Culture Notes

Generated from first-Friday second-candle review covering AAPL, MSFT, NVDA, and TSLA from 2026-01-02 through 2026-09-04.

This is research guidance, not production strategy logic.

## Core Takeaway

For stocks, the raw "fake-flip" label is not a trade trigger. It is a warning label.

Gold and major FX may reward immediate fake-flip reaction because news-release liquidity behaves differently. Stocks are more subdued and more structured. Single-stock opens also include opening auction effects, index/sector drag, and stock-specific flows.

The tradeable stock pattern is not:

```text
fake flip = enter
```

The better stock sequence is:

```text
initial impulse
  -> reclaim/rejection of open
  -> clean retest
  -> cross1/cross2 or micro-structure continuation
  -> trade
```

Sniping and ride-the-wave should be separate modes.

## Dynamic News Opening Markers

The useful "markers" during news openings are not necessarily the normal daily/F2 markers.

Use dynamic levels first:

- Opening price
- Initial impulse extreme
- Failed side of the opening move
- Reclaim/rejection of the open
- First pullback after reclaim/rejection
- Micro swing high/low after the retest
- Cross1 / Cross2 after the retest

## Symbol Culture Summary

### AAPL

Current read: sniper culture.

Observed traits:

- Most obedient of the four.
- Usually continues the first real direction.
- No true fake-flip cases in this batch.
- Earlier decision behavior than NVDA/TSLA.
- Better suited for small, consistent sniper trades than broad wave chasing.

Study metrics:

- Completed cases: 7
- Average 5-minute range: 0.97%
- Average absolute net move: 0.55%
- Fake-flip count: 0
- Wide same-way count: 5
- Average reversal timing: 15 seconds

Working interpretation:

AAPL may be useful for service-mode stability. It should not be forced into fake-flip logic. If AAPL gives a clean opening retest, take the sniper; otherwise skip or require much stronger confirmation.

### MSFT

Current read: sniper first, wave only with strong confirmation.

Observed traits:

- Cleaner than NVDA/TSLA, but can be deceptive.
- Lower visual chaos than NVDA/TSLA.
- Can provide clean retests, but follow-through may pause or mean-revert.
- Raw fake-flip is not enough.

Study metrics:

- Completed cases: 7
- Average 5-minute range: 0.97%
- Average absolute net move: 0.48%
- Fake-flip count: 1
- Wide same-way count: 3
- Average reversal timing: 14 seconds

Working interpretation:

MSFT should be treated conservatively. It can be traded as a sniper candidate. A wave trade needs acceptance after retest, preferably cross1/cross2 confirmation.

### NVDA

Current read: strongest ride-the-wave candidate.

Observed traits:

- More fake-looking opens.
- Can produce clean staircase continuation after direction is accepted.
- Needs confirmation; raw fake-flip is dangerous.
- Best example in this batch: NVDA 2026-05-01, where fake sell -> reclaim -> retest -> wave continuation was visually clear.

Study metrics:

- Completed cases: 7
- Average 5-minute range: 1.17%
- Average absolute net move: 0.45%
- Fake-flip count: 3
- Wide same-way count: 2
- Average reversal timing: 56 seconds

Working interpretation:

NVDA may be the first symbol to specialize in for a news-opening wave module. It should wait for reclaim/rejection, retest, and cross confirmation. Do not enter solely on fake-flip detection.

### TSLA

Current read: high-risk wave culture.

Observed traits:

- Biggest movement of the four.
- Can produce beautiful waves.
- Also produces late reversals and false positives.
- Best clean true fake-then-wave example: TSLA 2026-09-04.
- Bad false-positive example: TSLA 2026-06-05.

Study metrics:

- Completed cases: 7
- Average 5-minute range: 1.22%
- Average absolute net move: 0.48%
- Fake-flip count: 3
- Wide same-way count: 3
- Average reversal timing: 104 seconds

Working interpretation:

TSLA is profitable-looking but dangerous for autonomous service mode until filters are stronger. It may become valuable after NVDA-style wave rules are proven. Use stricter confirmation and tighter invalidation than NVDA.

## Mode Guidance

### Sniping Mode

Purpose:

- Higher win-rate target.
- Smaller but more consistent profit.
- Better fit for service mode.

Requirements:

- Clean retest/hold.
- Conservative entry.
- Fast exit.
- Avoid forcing trades on late fake-flip labels.

Best current symbols:

- AAPL
- MSFT with caution

### Ride-The-Wave Mode

Purpose:

- Lower frequency.
- Larger payoff.
- Useful when news opening creates a clear directional wave.

Requirements:

- Reclaim/rejection of open.
- Clean retest.
- Acceptance after retest.
- Cross1/cross2 or micro higher-high/lower-low confirmation.
- Structure-based stop, not blind hold.

Best current symbols:

- NVDA first
- TSLA later, after stricter filters

## Current Research Direction

The project does not need to master every symbol.

If we can specialize in one or two repeatable symbols, news trading becomes much more practical. The likely path is:

1. Keep core sniper mode as the service foundation.
2. Build a separate ride-the-wave study for NVDA.
3. Use TSLA only after NVDA rules are stable.
4. Treat AAPL/MSFT as conservative sniper candidates unless their wave structure is obvious.

The keeper idea is not "news trading works everywhere." The keeper idea is "each symbol has culture, and we only need one or two cultures we can read reliably."
